## Brand & Style

This design system embodies spiritual tranquility, sacred reverence, and refined modern craft. Designed specifically for an Arabic-first (RTL) mobile experience integrating Quranic reading and daily Azkar, the visual tone balances the sacred weight of classical Islamic manuscript tradition with the crisp minimalism of contemporary mobile interfaces.

### Core Philosophy
- **Reverence & Calm (Sakinah):** Surfaces evoke high-grade warm parchment, avoiding harsh modern blue-white glare to support prolonged, strain-free devotional reading.
- **Crafted Elegance (Ihsan):** Subtle metallic golds and deep forest emeralds invoke classical binding craft without decorative excess or distraction.
- **Clarity of Worship:** The UI recedes behind the sacred text; structural chrome, counters, and navigation remain respectful, precise, and visually silent until summoned.
- **RTL-Native Ergonomics:** Every transition, gesture, progress indicator, and layout flow is engineered primarily for Right-to-Left cognitive and thumb patterns.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from illuminated manuscript heritage: deep botanical greens, balanced warm golds, parchment tones, and deep charcoal calligraphy inks.

### Primary Roles
- **Primary (`#0A4D3C`):** Deep Emerald Green. Serves as the primary brand anchor for active prayer states, completed counter thresholds, primary action buttons, and dominant iconography.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`):** Warm Metallic Gold. Applied to sacred verse markers (Ayah end rosettes), progress rings, bookmark toggles, and delicate focal highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#063226`):** Rich Forest Emerald. Used for deep-surface headers, night-friendly container fills, and top navigational app bars.
- **Neutral (`#121816`):** Deep Charcoal. Delivers high-legibility typographic contrast without the stark harshness of pure black, pairing harmoniously with soft cream backdrops.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** Soft Cream / Warm Ivory (`#F9F7F2`) simulates archival vellum, filtering harsh backlight during dawn (Fajr) and late-night (Tahajjud) recitations.
- **Elevated Surfaces:** Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine gold-tinted borders for daily reading cards, surah index cards, and dhikr beads.
- **Border Accents:** Low-opacity gold lines (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.40)`) give tactile structure without heavy visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy strictly separates sacred canonical texts from operational UI labels:

1. **Sacred Arabic Script (Quranic & Azkar Text):**
   - Uses an authentic Uthmanic Naskh / Amiri font stack.
   - Generous vertical line-height (`lineHeight` up to `2.2x` font size) prevents complex Tashkeel (harakat, sukun, shaddah), Maddah, and Tajweed diacritics from overlapping or clipping.
   - Verse ends use native decorative glyph anchors containing Eastern Arabic numerals.

2. **Interface Chrome & Numerical Telemetry:**
   - Powered by modern Arabic sans-serif glyphs (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`).
   - Prioritizes clean stroke terminals, high geometric legibility, and uniform weight across varying density levels (e.g., Ayah indices, prayer times, search queries, circular counter rings).
   - Use Eastern Arabic numerals (`٠, ١, ٢, ٣...`) within devotional contexts and standard numerals in technical timestamps where appropriate.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-centric fluid rhythm tuned for one-handed thumb interaction in an RTL orientation.

### Grid & Structure
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 430px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Primary reading views stretch edge-to-edge with `1.25rem` inner horizontal padding to mimic page margins.
- **Foldables & Tablets (600px - 840px):** 8-column layout. Recitation cards center inside an elevated 640px maximum reading container with bilateral metadata panels (Tafsir, translation, audio reciter selection).
- **RTL Geometry:** Grid flow, modal drawers, and list items build right-to-left. Floating action buttons (such as the Tasbih tap target) anchor to the bottom-left or centered bottom region to balance one-handed thumb ergonomics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows, relying instead on warm ambient illumination, soft card layering, and metallic boundaries reminiscent of gilded parchment edges.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base background in `#F9F7F2` (Warm Ivory). Completely flat, matte, and non-reflective.
- **Level 1 (Card Surfaces):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards resting atop Level 0. Raised by an ultra-soft amber-tinted shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(10, 77, 60, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`) and encased in a 1px border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.20)`).
- **Level 2 (Modals & Sticky Headers):** Deep Forest Emerald (`#063226`) with a subtle top hairline border of solid metallic gold (`#D4AF37`). Background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 92% opacity ensures sacred text beneath is gracefully obscured.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Floating Accents):** Tasbih counter pads and audio floating sheets use `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(6, 50, 38, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs warm, softened geometric profiles (`roundedness: 2`, where base components use `0.5rem` to `1rem`, and primary content cards scale up to `1rem - 1.5rem`). This removes aggressive sharp edges and produces an organic, handheld, tablet-of-remembrance feel.

### Geometric Embellishments
- **Surah Headers & Rosettes:** Integrate subtle octagonal or 8-point Islamic star (Khatam) silhouettes as icon containers and Ayah completion badges.
- **Circular Indicators:** Progress indicators for daily Dhikr goals and Hizb/Juz completion use uninterrupted 360-degree circular pill arcs with hairline gold strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled Deep Emerald Green (`#0A4D3C`) with Pristine White text and subtle gold border glow on focus. Minimum height 48px, rounded to 16px (`rounded-lg`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px gold border (`#D4AF37`), Deep Forest Emerald text.
- **Sacred Audio Player Buttons:** Circular buttons (`w-12 h-12` up to `w-16 h-16`) with concentric metallic ring indicators.

### Cards (Surah & Azkar Units)
- Structured with White (`#FFFFFF`) fill, 16px to 24px corner radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), and 1px border (`border-amber-300/30`).
- Headers feature the Surah title in prominent calligraphy alongside revelation place badge (Makki/Madani) and verse tally.
- Verse body cards provide ample line spacing, followed by contextual bottom icon ribbons: Tafsir, Audio Play, Bookmark, and Share.

### Tasbih & Counter Rings
- Circular progress track in muted gold-tinted cream (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`) with an active stroke in Warm Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`).
- Central large display showing the count in crisp Eastern Arabic numerals with a generous thumb-tap target area providing subtle haptic confirmation.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Soft pill shapes (`rounded-full`) in 10% opacity Emerald (`rgba(10, 77, 60, 0.10)`) with Deep Emerald text, denoting Juz numbers, Hizb quarters, and repeat counts (e.g., `٣ مرات`).
- **Category Chips:** Horizontal scrolling pill filters for Hisni Azkar categories (Morning, Evening, Travel, Sleep) with gold outline states for active selections.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background in Pristine White, inset 1px gold border, right-aligned search glyph, and soft placeholder text in neutral deep charcoal at 50% opacity.
- Rounded 16px corners (`rounded-lg`) maintaining visual cohesion with reading cards.
